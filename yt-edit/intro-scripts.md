## Mandarin tracker

Some people asked me for app ideas to build live, so I picked one: a tracker for how much time a friend spends on Mandarin. That means YouTube videos, reading in Readwise and Pleco, and chatting with AI in Mandarin, on a Mac and an Android phone.

I wrote one long prompt and let Claude Code make almost every decision. It came back with a plan, I answered a few questions, and then it built in the background while I worked on other things. You'll see that as short cards.

At the end it installs an app on my phone that reads what's on screen, and Claude tests it on the phone itself.

What worked best: a goal I could check, and asking for "the closest you can get" instead of telling it how.

## German learning app

I want to learn German, so I built the app I'd want to use. The first time I tried this, I jumped straight to "build me an app" and drowned in small decisions.

This time I started by asking what the evidence says works: spaced repetition, lots of reading and listening, and speaking with feedback. Then I asked Claude to copy the best existing tool for each of those, put them all in one app that shares what I know, and show me a mock-up before building.

In the afternoon I try it: review cards, stories written from words I know, podcasts, German subtitles on Netflix, and speaking practice with an AI.

What worked best: evidence first, copying what already works, and a mock-up before the real build.

## Both apps

This is one day of building two language apps with Claude Code at the same time. One tracks how much Mandarin a friend studies across a Mac and a phone. The other is a German learning app for me.

They needed opposite approaches. For the tracker, the goal was clear, so I wrote one prompt and let Claude decide everything. For the German app, I didn't know what good looked like, so I started from the research and asked for a mock-up before the real build.

While Claude works on one, I switch to the other. The video follows the real order, so you'll see me jumping back and forth.

What worked best: give Claude a goal it can check itself, and run several things in parallel so you're never just waiting.

## Full day

This is a full day of building with Claude Code, from about 11:30 to 3:40, cut down but in the real order. I worked on four things.

Two worked well: a Mandarin time tracker for a friend and a German learning app for me. One didn't: an auditable AI reviewer for research papers.
