# screen-recordings

Record with the menu bar app (`Sources/`, `./build.sh install`), then cut the YouTube video with `yt-edit/` (steps in its README).

## Last video, and the default for the next one

10 Nov 2026: [Making language apps for friends | Alejo & his Claude Code](https://youtu.be/mL-v3CrcM_w), public, 65 min.

- One real-time cut of the projects I'm showing: everything in order, speech at real speed. Silences of 3 s or more play at 4×, and 6 s or more at 8×. Other work is a labelled time-lapse, with a card between recordings.
- Private text (email, billing, tokens, private links, other people's data) is pixelated; Slack stays visible.
- Audio: mic +18 dB, computer audio −10 dB, limiter at −1 dB.
- Uploaded in YouTube Studio with `out/<cut>.description.txt`, category Science & Technology, not made for kids.
